# P07 Literature Coverage

The frozen P07 comparison matrix contains **22 rows** and is exposed here as
`P07_CORE_LITERATURE_MATRIX.csv`.

The broader saturation review explicitly screened **30 studies**, tracked in
`P07_SCREENED_STUDY_REGISTRY.csv`.

The registry is a coverage audit, not a replacement bibliography. For supporting studies
that were screened but not promoted into the core comparison matrix, bibliographic
metadata must be taken from the original publication rather than inferred from this file.

## Frozen research-gap interpretation

The claimed contribution is **not** partial-window SOH estimation, unseen-cell validation,
condition-shift analysis, or MCU deployment individually. It is the joint evaluation of a
fixed nominal 10-s causal V/I/T observation, strict all-battery leave-one-out generalization,
adaptation-free held-out operating-condition tests with matched degradation penalties, and
measured physical ESP32-C6 execution.
